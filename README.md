**EMBERLINE** — a round-based tactical FPS in a dusk rail yard. You are the lone attacker of team EMBER against four ASHEN defenders: plant the spark charge at Foundry (A) or Depot (B), or eliminate the whole defence. First to 5 rounds takes the yard. Money earned each round buys a carbine, an SMG, grenades and armour in the buy phase; bots hold angles, patrol the lanes, react to your gunfire and move to defuse a planted charge. Built on three.js 0.187.0, single-player vs bots, no network, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click to deploy, WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R reload, 1/2/3 weapons, G grenade, E plant (hold inside a site ring), B buy menu, C crouch, Space jump.
